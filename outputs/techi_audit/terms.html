02

Who can have an account

You need to be at least 16 to make a TECHi account. To subscribe to a paid analyst, you need to be 18 — paying for stuff requires the legal capacity to enter a contract. A learner younger than 16 does not get an account. A parent or guardian who meets the age rule owns the account, pays, and is the contact for any TECHi Learn course that child takes.

One person, one account. Don’t share logins. If we believe an account is shared between multiple people, we may suspend it.

We can refuse new sign-ups or close existing accounts at our discretion, mostly for the obvious reasons (impersonation, spam, abuse, fraud). When we do, we’ll explain why unless explaining would tip off bad actors.

03

TECHi Learn

These terms cover TECHi Learn. A course purchase is a contract with TechAbout LLC, paid through Stripe. Enrollment opens only while a cohort is actually accepting seats. Buying a seat does not create a child account.

  • Course purchases. The price, cohort dates, and what the seat includes are the ones shown at checkout. A guardian buys a seat for a child learner and remains the customer.
  • Refunds. You can ask for a refund before the cohort starts. After it starts, refunds are limited to a seat we cannot deliver. Stripe posts any refund to the original payment method.
  • Cancellations and rescheduling. We may cancel or move a cohort. If we cancel, you get a refund of what you paid for that seat or a move to the next cohort you accept. If you need to reschedule, email us before the cohort starts and we will move the seat when another cohort has room.
  • Access period. Course materials stay available to the paying account through the cohort end date shown at checkout, plus 30 days, unless we tell you a different window on the course page.
  • Certificates. A certificate is a record that the named learner finished the published lessons. It is not a degree, a license, or a job offer. It stays private unless the guardian allows it to be shown.
  • Instructors. We can change an instructor. The course description is the promise, not a named teacher.
  • Guardians and minors. A child learner does not log in. The guardian account holds consent, payment, and messages. Classroom conduct rules apply to whoever is in the session, including a guardian sitting with a child.
  • Classroom behavior. No harassment, no sharing other learners’ names or work outside the cohort, and no recording a session yourself.
  • Recorded sessions. If we record a class, we say so before it starts. The recording is for enrolled accounts in that cohort. We do not sell it.
  • Intellectual property. Lesson materials, exercises, and recordings belong to TechAbout LLC or the instructor who licensed them to us. You get a personal, non-transferable right to use them for the access period. You don’t get the right to republish them.
  • Educational disclaimer. Learn is education, not investment advice, a professional certification, or a promise of employment or trading results.

Comments, two-take responses, analyst reports, applications, watchlist names — anything you submit while logged in is yours. You keep the copyright.

What you give us is permission to use it: a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to display your content on TECHi, archive it, and (for comments and reports) include it in newsletter digests or social-media posts that promote your contribution. The licence ends when you delete the content, with one carve-out: anything we’ve already shown in a newsletter or social post stays archived where it was sent.

Don’t post anything you don’t have the right to post. If we receive a credible takedown notice, we’ll honour it.

05

What you can’t do

Most of these are common sense. We’re writing them down so there’s no confusion when we have to enforce them.

  • No automated scraping. You can read TECHi with your eyes and your browser. You can’t hit the API or pages with a bot to bulk- harvest content. If you’re building a research tool and want a feed, email us.
  • No reverse-engineering. Don’t try to extract source code, private API endpoints, or proprietary models from the site.
  • No impersonating other people. Especially not financial advisors, regulators, or TECHi staff.
  • No market manipulation. Don’t use TECHi to coordinate pump-and-dumps, post material non-public information, or otherwise commit securities fraud. If we even suspect it, we’ll cooperate fully with regulators.
  • No spam, harassment, hate speech, or doxxing. See our comments policy for the long form.
06

Paid subscriptions

If you subscribe to a TECHi analyst or to a TECHi premium tier, here’s how billing works:

  • Subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing period (monthly or annually, depending on the plan you picked) until you cancel.
  • You can cancel any time from Settings → Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period — we don’t pro-rate refunds for partial months.
  • We use Stripe for payments. Your card data never touches our servers. Stripe’s terms also apply to the card-handling part.
  • If a charge fails, we’ll retry up to four times across two weeks before suspending access. You’ll get an email each time.
  • We can change pricing on new sign-ups any time. For existing subscribers, we’ll give 30 days’ notice before a price increase takes effect and you can cancel before it does.
